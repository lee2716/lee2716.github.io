I am a **Research Fellow** at the [University of Bologna](https://www.unibo.it/en), in the PULP Lab, part of the [PULP Platform](https://pulp-platform.org/) run jointly with ETH Zürich. I work with Prof. Francesco Conti on the deployment of AI workloads on heterogeneous parallel many-core architectures.

I received my **M.Sc. in Telecommunications Engineering** from the University of Bologna in March 2026. My thesis, *Tiny LLM Automatic Deployment for RISC-V-Based High-Efficiency Compute Cluster Platforms*, extended the open-source [Deeploy](https://github.com/pulp-platform/Deeploy) compiler so that a small transformer language model runs on an eight-core RISC-V cluster with 128 KiB of scratchpad memory.

## Research interests

Hardware–software co-design for edge AI on RISC-V platforms:

* neural-network compilers and the deployment of transformer and small language models;
* cycle-level modelling of heterogeneous many-core and in-memory-computing accelerators;
* embedded system software: device drivers and real-time operating systems.

## Open-source work

* **[Deeploy #153](https://github.com/pulp-platform/Deeploy/pull/153): FP32 operators and tiling support for MicroLlama on Snitch.** My thesis work, merged in August 2026 into Deeploy, the compiler jointly maintained by ETH Zürich and the University of Bologna.
* **[Accelerator Phylogeny](https://lee2716.github.io/accelerator-phylogeny/).** An interactive evolutionary tree of AI accelerators.
